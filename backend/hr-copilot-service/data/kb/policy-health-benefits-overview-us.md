Health Benefits Overview - United States
Domain: Benefits
Country: United States (US)
Effective date: 2026-07-07
Status: Current
Employees in United States are eligible for company-sponsored health coverage from their start date, with
enrollment completed within the first 30 days of employment.
Coverage tiers include Employee Only, Employee + Spouse, and Employee + Family; premiums are
co-funded by the employee via payroll deduction.
Outside of the annual open enrollment window, coverage changes require a qualifying life event (marriage,
birth/adoption, loss of other coverage).

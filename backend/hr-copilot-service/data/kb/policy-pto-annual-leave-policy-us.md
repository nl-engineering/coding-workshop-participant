PTO / Annual Leave Policy - United States
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: United States (US)
Effective date: 2026-02-20
Status: Current
Employees in United States accrue 15 days of paid time off per year, increasing to 20 days per year at
Director level and above.
PTO accrues monthly and may be requested in half-day increments, subject to manager approval.
Unused PTO carryover into the next calendar year is capped at 5 days unless local law requires otherwise.

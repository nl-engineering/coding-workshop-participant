Payroll Deductions - United States
Domain: Payroll
Country: United States (US)
Effective date: 2026-03-17
Status: Current
Standard deductions in United States include statutory tax withholding, 401(k) with 4% employer match, and
any elected benefits contributions.
One-time deductions (e.g., equipment recovery, salary advance repayment) must be authorized in writing by
the employee before being applied to a pay cycle.
Disputed deductions are reversed on the next pay cycle once HR Operations confirms the error.

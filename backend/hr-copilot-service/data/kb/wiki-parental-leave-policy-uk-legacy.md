# Parental Leave Policy - United Kingdom

> Domain: Time & Absence
> Country: United Kingdom (UK)
> Last updated: 2022-07-05
> Status: Unreviewed (legacy)

⚠️ *This page was migrated from the old intranet and has not been reviewed since 2022-07-05. It may not reflect the current policy PDF.*

Employees in United Kingdom are entitled to 48 weeks of parental leave: 39 weeks paid statutory leave, remaining 13 weeks unpaid.

Employees should notify HR Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date to begin the parental leave case.

Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable statutory leave in the employee's country.

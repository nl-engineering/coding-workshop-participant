Parental Leave Policy - United States
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: United States (US)
Effective date: 2026-02-18
Status: Current
Employees in United States are entitled to 12 weeks of parental leave: 6 weeks paid company benefit,
remainder unpaid job-protected leave (FMLA).
Employees should notify HR Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date to begin the
parental leave case.
Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable statutory leave in the employee's country.

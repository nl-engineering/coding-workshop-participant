Parental Leave Policy - Australia
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: Australia (AU)
Effective date: 2025-12-09
Status: Current
Employees in Australia are entitled to 18 weeks of parental leave: 18 weeks government Parental Leave Pay
plus 6 weeks employer top-up.
Employees should notify HR Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date to begin the
parental leave case.
Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable statutory leave in the employee's country.

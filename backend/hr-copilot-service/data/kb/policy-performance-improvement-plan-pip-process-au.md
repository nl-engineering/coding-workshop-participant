Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Process - Australia
Domain: Performance Management
Country: Australia (AU)
Effective date: 2025-10-09
Status: Current
A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is initiated by a manager, in partnership with HR Operations, when
an employee's performance falls materially below expectations.
A standard PIP runs 90 days and must include specific, measurable goals, the support/resources provided,
and a defined review cadence (typically every 2 weeks).
All PIP documentation, feedback, and outcomes must be logged for audit purposes; verbal-only PIPs are not
permitted.

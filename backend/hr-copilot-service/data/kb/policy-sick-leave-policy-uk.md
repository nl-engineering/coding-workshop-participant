Sick Leave Policy - United Kingdom
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: United Kingdom (UK)
Effective date: 2026-07-28
Status: Current
Employees in United Kingdom are covered by Statutory Sick Pay from the fourth consecutive day of illness,
in addition to any company sick leave benefit.
A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.
Sick leave balances (where applicable) reset at the start of each calendar year and do not carry over.

# Manager Reassignment Runbook

> Domain: Employee/Manager Data Changes
> Audience: HR Operations analysts

**Domain:** Employee/Manager Data Changes



## Purpose



Step-by-step procedure for: Manager Reassignment Runbook.



## Steps



1. Verify the request comes from the employee's current manager, HRBP, or the new manager with HRBP sign-off.

2. Confirm the new manager exists in the org hierarchy and is not a direct report of the employee being moved.

3. Update the manager_id in the employee record and log the effective date.

4. Trigger downstream notifications (payroll, benefits, IT access review) for the org change.

5. Confirm the change is reflected in the org chart within 1 business day.



## Escalation



Escalate to HR Operations leadership if the reassignment crosses a legal entity or country.

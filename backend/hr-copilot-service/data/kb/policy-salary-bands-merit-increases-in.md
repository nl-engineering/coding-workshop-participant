Salary Bands & Merit Increases - India
Domain: Compensation
Country: India (IN)
Effective date: 2026-02-22
Status: Current
Compensation in India is set against published salary bands per job level, denominated in INR.
Merit increases are awarded annually as part of the compensation review cycle and are informed by
performance rating, position in band, and local market data.
Off-cycle adjustments require Compensation team approval and a documented business justification.

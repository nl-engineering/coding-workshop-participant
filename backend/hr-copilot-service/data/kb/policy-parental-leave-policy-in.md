Parental Leave Policy - India
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: India (IN)
Effective date: 2025-11-20
Status: Current
Employees in India are entitled to 26 weeks of parental leave: 26 weeks paid maternity leave; 1 week
paternity leave.
Employees should notify HR Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date to begin the
parental leave case.
Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable statutory leave in the employee's country.

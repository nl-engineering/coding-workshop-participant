# Org Health Reporting Guide

> Domain: Misc
> Audience: HR Operations analysts

**Domain:** Misc



## Purpose



Step-by-step procedure for: Org Health Reporting Guide.



## Steps



1. Collect the reference employee's profile as a template (level, department, location, compensation band).

2. Confirm headcount approval exists for the new requisition before creating it.

3. Adapt the template for the target country's employment terms and local compliance requirements.

4. Route the requisition for Finance and HRBP approval before publishing externally.



## Escalation



Escalate to Talent Acquisition leadership if approval is not received within 5 business days.

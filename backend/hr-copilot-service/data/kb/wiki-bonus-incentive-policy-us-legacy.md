# Bonus & Incentive Policy - United States

> Domain: Compensation

> Last updated: 2022-04-11
> Status: Unreviewed (legacy)

⚠️ *This page was migrated from the old intranet and has not been reviewed since 2022-04-11. It may not reflect the current policy PDF.*

Eligible employees participate in an annual bonus program tied to individual and company performance against plan.

Bonus payouts are pro-rated for employees who joined, transferred, or went on extended leave during the plan year.

Bonus amounts are confidential and communicated individually; they are not discussed in group settings.

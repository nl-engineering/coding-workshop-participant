# Direct Deposit & Bank Changes - United Kingdom

> Domain: Payroll
> Country: United Kingdom (UK)
> Last updated: 2024-02-14
> Status: Unreviewed (legacy)

⚠️ *This page was migrated from the old intranet and has not been reviewed since 2024-02-14. It may not reflect the current policy PDF.*

Employees in United Kingdom are paid via direct deposit in GBP to a single bank account.

Bank detail changes submitted before the payroll cutoff (5 business days before pay date) take effect on the next pay cycle; changes submitted after cutoff take effect the following cycle.

For security, bank changes require a secondary confirmation (email or SMS) before being applied.

# Public Holiday Calendar - India

> Domain: Time & Absence
> Country: India (IN)
> Last updated: 2023-10-17
> Status: Unreviewed (legacy)

⚠️ *This page was migrated from the old intranet and has not been reviewed since 2023-10-17. It may not reflect the current policy PDF.*

India observes 11 paid public holidays per year, published annually by HR Operations ahead of the new calendar year.

Employees who work on a public holiday are entitled to a compensating day off or holiday premium pay, per local requirements.

The published calendar is the source of truth; regional office calendars should not be relied on for payroll purposes.

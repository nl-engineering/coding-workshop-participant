Public Holiday Calendar - United States
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: United States (US)
Effective date: 2026-02-19
Status: Current
United States observes 10 paid public holidays per year, published annually by HR Operations ahead of the
new calendar year.
Employees who work on a public holiday are entitled to a compensating day off or holiday premium pay, per
local requirements.
The published calendar is the source of truth; regional office calendars should not be relied on for payroll
purposes.

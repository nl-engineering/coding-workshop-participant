Parental Leave Policy - United Kingdom
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: United Kingdom (UK)
Effective date: 2025-09-09
Status: Current
Employees in United Kingdom are entitled to 52 weeks of parental leave: 39 weeks paid statutory leave,
remaining 13 weeks unpaid.
Employees should notify HR Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date to begin the
parental leave case.
Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable statutory leave in the employee's country.

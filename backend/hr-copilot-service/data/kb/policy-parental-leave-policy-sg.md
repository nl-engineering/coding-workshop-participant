Parental Leave Policy - Singapore
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: Singapore (SG)
Effective date: 2026-07-29
Status: Current
Employees in Singapore are entitled to 16 weeks of parental leave: Government-Paid Maternity Leave; 2
weeks Government-Paid Paternity Leave.
Employees should notify HR Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date to begin the
parental leave case.
Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable statutory leave in the employee's country.

# Health Benefits Overview - United States

> Domain: Benefits
> Country: United States (US)
> Last updated: 2023-08-08
> Status: Unreviewed (legacy)

⚠️ *This page was migrated from the old intranet and has not been reviewed since 2023-08-08. It may not reflect the current policy PDF.*

Employees in United States are eligible for company-sponsored health coverage from their start date, with enrollment completed within the first 30 days of employment.

Coverage tiers include Employee Only, Employee + Spouse, and Employee + Family; premiums are co-funded by the employee via payroll deduction.

Outside of the annual open enrollment window, coverage changes require a qualifying life event (marriage, birth/adoption, loss of other coverage).

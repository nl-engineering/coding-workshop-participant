Bonus & Incentive Policy - Ireland
Domain: Compensation
Country: Ireland (IE)
Effective date: 2025-11-21
Status: Current
Eligible employees participate in an annual bonus program tied to individual and company performance
against plan.
Bonus payouts are pro-rated for employees who joined, transferred, or went on extended leave during the
plan year.
Bonus amounts are confidential and communicated individually; they are not discussed in group settings.

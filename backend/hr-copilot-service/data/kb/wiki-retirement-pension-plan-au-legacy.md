# Retirement / Pension Plan - Australia

> Country: Australia (AU)
> Last updated: 2022-10-12
> Status: Unreviewed (legacy)

⚠️ *This page was migrated from the old intranet and has not been reviewed since 2022-10-12. It may not reflect the current policy PDF.*

Employees in Australia participate in Superannuation Guarantee at 11%.

Enrollment is automatic for eligible employees, with the option to opt out or adjust contribution levels through the HR portal.

Employer contributions vest according to local statutory requirements.

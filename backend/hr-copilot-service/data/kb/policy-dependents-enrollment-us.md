Dependents & Enrollment - United States
Domain: Benefits
Country: United States (US)
Effective date: 2026-03-30
Status: Current
Employees may add or remove dependents (spouse, domestic partner, children) from their benefits coverage
through the self-service HR portal.
Adding a dependent requires supporting documentation (e.g., marriage or birth certificate) uploaded at the
time of the request.
Dependent changes take effect on the first day of the month following approval, except for newborns, which
are covered retroactively to the date of birth.

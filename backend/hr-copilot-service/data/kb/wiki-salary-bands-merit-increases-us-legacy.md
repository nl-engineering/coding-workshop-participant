# Salary Bands & Merit Increases - United States

> Domain: Compensation
> Country: United States (US)
> Last updated: 2024-01-17
> Status: Unreviewed (legacy)

⚠️ *This page was migrated from the old intranet and has not been reviewed since 2024-01-17. It may not reflect the current policy PDF.*

Compensation in United States is set against published salary bands per job level, denominated in USD.

Merit increases are awarded annually as part of the compensation review cycle and are informed by performance rating, position in band, and local market data.

Off-cycle adjustments require Compensation team approval and a documented business justification.

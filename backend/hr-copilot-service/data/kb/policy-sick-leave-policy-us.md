Sick Leave Policy - United States
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: United States (US)
Effective date: 2025-12-05
Status: Current
Employees in United States are entitled to 8 paid sick days per year.
A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.
Sick leave balances (where applicable) reset at the start of each calendar year and do not carry over.

Parental Leave Policy - Ireland
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: Ireland (IE)
Effective date: 2025-10-03
Status: Current
Employees in Ireland are entitled to 26 weeks of parental leave: 26 weeks paid maternity leave (state
benefit); 2 weeks paid paternity leave.
Employees should notify HR Operations at least 8 weeks before the expected leave start date to begin the
parental leave case.
Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable statutory leave in the employee's country.

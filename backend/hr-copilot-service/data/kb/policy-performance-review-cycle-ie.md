Performance Review Cycle - Ireland
Domain: Performance Management
Country: Ireland (IE)
Effective date: 2025-11-30
Status: Current
Performance reviews run on a semi-annual cycle, combining manager assessment, peer feedback, and
self-assessment.
Ratings feed into the compensation review cycle and must be finalized by the published cutoff date each
cycle.
Employees under a Performance Improvement Plan are reviewed on a separate, shorter cadence defined by
that plan.

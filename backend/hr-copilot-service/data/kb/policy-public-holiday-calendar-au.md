Public Holiday Calendar - Australia
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: Australia (AU)
Effective date: 2025-09-30
Status: Current
Australia observes 10 paid public holidays per year, published annually by HR Operations ahead of the new
calendar year.
Employees who work on a public holiday are entitled to a compensating day off or holiday premium pay, per
local requirements.
The published calendar is the source of truth; regional office calendars should not be relied on for payroll
purposes.

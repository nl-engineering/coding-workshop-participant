# Performance Review Cycle - Ireland

> Domain: Performance Management
> Country: Ireland (IE)
> Last updated: 2022-10-17
> Status: Unreviewed (legacy)

⚠️ *This page was migrated from the old intranet and has not been reviewed since 2022-10-17. It may not reflect the current policy PDF.*

Performance reviews run on a semi-annual cycle, combining manager assessment, peer feedback, and self-assessment.

Ratings feed into the compensation review cycle and must be finalized by the published cutoff date each cycle.

Employees under a Performance Improvement Plan are reviewed on a separate, shorter cadence defined by that plan.

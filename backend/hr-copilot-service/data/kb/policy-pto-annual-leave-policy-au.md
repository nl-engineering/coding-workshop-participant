PTO / Annual Leave Policy - Australia
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: Australia (AU)
Effective date: 2026-06-04
Status: Current
Employees in Australia accrue 20 days of paid time off per year, increasing to 24 days per year at Director
level and above.
PTO accrues monthly and may be requested in half-day increments, subject to manager approval.
Unused PTO carryover into the next calendar year is capped at 5 days unless local law requires otherwise.

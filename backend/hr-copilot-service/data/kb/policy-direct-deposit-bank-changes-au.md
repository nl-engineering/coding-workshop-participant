Direct Deposit & Bank Changes - Australia
Domain: Payroll
Country: Australia (AU)
Effective date: 2026-03-10
Status: Current
Employees in Australia are paid via direct deposit in AUD to a single bank account.
Bank detail changes submitted before the payroll cutoff (5 business days before pay date) take effect on the
next pay cycle; changes submitted after cutoff take effect the following cycle.
For security, bank changes require a secondary confirmation (email or SMS) before being applied.

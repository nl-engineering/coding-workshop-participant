Payroll Deductions - United Kingdom
Domain: Payroll
Country: United Kingdom (UK)
Effective date: 2025-09-13
Status: Current
Standard deductions in United Kingdom include statutory tax withholding, Auto-enrolment workplace pension,
3% employer contribution, and any elected benefits contributions.
One-time deductions (e.g., equipment recovery, salary advance repayment) must be authorized in writing by
the employee before being applied to a pay cycle.
Disputed deductions are reversed on the next pay cycle once HR Operations confirms the error.

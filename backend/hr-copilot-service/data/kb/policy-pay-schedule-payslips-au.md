Pay Schedule & Payslips - Australia
Domain: Payroll
Country: Australia (AU)
Effective date: 2025-09-27
Status: Current
Employees in Australia are paid on a fortnightly basis in AUD.
Payslips are issued electronically no later than two business days before the pay date and include gross pay,
itemized deductions, employer contributions, and year-to-date totals.
Employees who do not receive a payslip, or who believe their pay is incorrect, should raise a case with HR
Operations within 30 days of the affected pay date.

PTO / Annual Leave Policy - India
Domain: Time & Absence
Country: India (IN)
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Status: Current
Employees in India accrue 18 days of paid time off per year, increasing to 24 days per year at Director level
and above.
PTO accrues monthly and may be requested in half-day increments, subject to manager approval.
Unused PTO carryover into the next calendar year is capped at 5 days unless local law requires otherwise.
